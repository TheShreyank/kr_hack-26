How to use Chitra-Maathu

  1. Search by voice. Press Speak, say what you want in Kannada (for example "ದೇವಸ್ಥಾನದ ಹೂವಿನ ಅಂಗಡಿ ತೋರಿಸು"), then press it again to stop. Matching photos appear below.
  2. Or type / filter by category. Write your search in Kannada or English and press Search, tap one of the example chips, or filter by scene category (Streets, Food, Festivals, Culture, Nature, Building).
  3. Listen. Press Play Audio on any photo to hear its spoken Kannada description.
  4. Add your own photo triplet. Press Add Photo, upload an image (drag on the image if you need to blur faces or number plates), record a 15–30 s spoken description in Kannada, pick the district, category, and Speaker ID, then save.
  5. Download the Metadata CSV. Press Metadata CSV at the top to export every triplet row (place, category, speaker ID, photo, audio, and caption).
